About

What Started It All

I am an avid collector of official and custom LEGO. As I collected more I noticed that there wasn't enough figures of characters I enjoyed in the styles that I wanted. So I took it to myself to design and make said figures.

Focus On Enjoyment

As a collector, I pride myself in having a nice display for everything that I own. Thus, the custom figure I make has a plethora of additional goodies such as stickers, displays, carrying cases, and custom pieces.

A Blend Of Classic And Modern

I enjoy classic LEGO minifigures, but the joy of modern figures leave something wanted for my classic collection. My minifigures take a mix of classic and modern designs to fully incorporate my favorite aspects of both types of minifigures.
